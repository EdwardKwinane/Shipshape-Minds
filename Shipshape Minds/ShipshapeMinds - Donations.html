Donation FAQs

Can I donate from outside the country?

Yes, we accept international donations via credit card and PayPal. The amount will be converted at your bank's current rate.

How can I cancel a monthly donation?

You can cancel your monthly recurring donation at any time by contacting our support team or through your user portal.

Will I receive a tax certificate?

Absolutely. We issue Section 18A tax certificates for all donations over R10 made during the financial year.
